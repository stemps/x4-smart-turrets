# Changelog

## Unreleased

- "Attack best-suited target" option in the "All turrets" mode dropdown of the
  map's ship info and the cockpit's ship interactions menu: fast-turning turrets with
  fast projectiles or beams, and turrets with area damage, attack fighters first; all
  others attack capital ships first. Missile turrets go by size (M fighters, L/XL
  capital ships).
- Thresholds (minimum turn rate, minimum projectile speed, minimum turn rate with area
  damage) are adjustable on the mod's page in the "Extension Options" menu and stored
  per user profile. A "View results" button opens a page that lists every turret by the
  role it gets with the current thresholds, and "Reset to defaults" restores them. Requires SirNukes Mod Support APIs.
